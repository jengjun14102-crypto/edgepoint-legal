Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-09-09

EdgePoint is a friendly place for tennis fans to predict matches and talk about the sport. To keep it welcoming, everyone must follow these guidelines when commenting or interacting.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or personal attacks.
  • No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any protected characteristic.
  • Disagree with ideas, not with people.

Keep it clean and legal

  • No sexual, violent, shocking or otherwise inappropriate content.
  • No illegal content or activity.
  • No spam, scams, phishing or misleading links.
  • No impersonation of players, staff or other users.

No gambling promotion

  • Do not post bookmaker links, betting tips, odds, affiliate links, or promote real-money gambling in any form. EdgePoint is a prediction game, not a betting service.

Respect privacy & ownership

  • Do not share other people's private information.
  • Only post content you have the right to share.

Reporting & enforcement

If you see content that breaks these rules:

  • Report a comment, or report / block a user, directly in the App (long-press a comment or open the options menu).
  • Our team reviews reports and may remove content, issue warnings, or suspend/terminate accounts.
  • Blocking a user hides their content from you immediately.

We aim to review reports promptly. Serious violations may result in an immediate ban.

Contact

Questions or appeals: support@edgepointtennis.com
