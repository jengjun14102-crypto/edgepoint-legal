Delete Your Account

Last updated: 2026-09-09

You can permanently delete your EdgePoint Tennis Community account and its associated data. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  • Open the App and sign in.
  • Go to Settings → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By request

If you cannot access the App, email support@edgepointtennis.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your account (email, display name, password hash)
  • Your predictions and prediction history
  • Your comments, likes and reports
  • Your follows and blocks
  • Your badges, XP, points and streak history
  • Your notifications and Premium unlock records held by the App
Premium subscriptions are separate. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop billing, also cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Data we may retain

We may keep a limited set of records where required by law or for legitimate accounting and fraud-prevention purposes (for example, that a purchase occurred), retained only for as long as legally required and kept separate from your deleted profile.
